Overview

The pulse of what you're responsible for — one screen instead of six tabs. Scoped to your access, same as everywhere else.

Search Seekers

Find any seeker on record and open their full profile.

Organisation and team management

Structure lives in Seva Departments — the org chart, adding departments and roles. Team Management is where people are added to and removed from roles.

Satsang Management

Definitions go Draft → In Review → Approved before events can be scheduled off them. Each event still needs a conductor's approval before it's confirmed.

Form Management System

Definitions go Draft \u2192 In Review \u2192 Published before a form is live. Submissions themselves are still reviewed from My Seva Activities, not here.

Ticker Management

Notifications that scroll across the top of every screen, for everyone signed in \u2014 not scoped to a single tab.

My Seva Activities

Tasks you have taken on, what the system is raising — contact overdue against the fixed window and sensitivity flags — and form submissions awaiting review before they become seeker records.

Spiritual Journey

Where every seeker sits, and what brought them in. Not a sales pipeline — there is no close, and moving backwards is ordinary.

Calendar

Satsangs and retreats already recorded in the Satsang Conducting System. This is a view over that data, not a second copy of it.

Satsang reporting

Every satsang that a curious person or seeker attends must be reported by the conductor. This is that discipline, made visible rather than assumed.

Seva Management

Matching seekers to service, by skill and by capacity — not just a list of who volunteered once.

Dashboard

Live counts from Postgres — nothing here is illustrative.

Operations

What the technical owner looks at each morning. Everything here is machine-generated — nobody maintains it by hand.
